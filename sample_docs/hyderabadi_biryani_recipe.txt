Ammi's Hyderabadi chicken biryani (the one from the family group)

Serves 6. Total time about 2 hours.

Marinate 1 kg chicken overnight in thick curd, ginger-garlic paste, fried
onions, green chillies, mint, coriander, turmeric, red chilli powder and a
squeeze of lime. Soak 750 g aged basmati rice for 30 minutes.

Boil the rice with whole spices - bay leaf, cardamom, cloves, cinnamon and
shahi jeera - until it is 70 percent done, then drain. Layer the marinated
chicken at the bottom of a heavy handi, cover with the parboiled basmati,
and top with saffron soaked in warm milk, more fried onions, mint and a
spoon of ghee.

Seal the lid with atta dough and cook on dum: 10 minutes on high flame, then
25 minutes on the lowest flame with a tawa underneath. Rest 15 minutes before
opening. Serve with mirchi ka salan and dahi ki chutney.

Note from Ammi: do not skip the overnight marination, and never stir the pot
after layering.
